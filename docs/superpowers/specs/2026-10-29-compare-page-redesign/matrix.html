What changed

  • Blue verified tick for yes, a grey tick for partly, a quiet dash for no. The blue is #1a8cd8 in light (3.6:1 against white) and #1d9bf0 in dark. Words carry the meaning, so the colour is never the only signal.
  • No wrapping. Every phrase fits one line: 18 characters at most for the rivals, 24 for us. The build fails if one runs over. Detail lives in a row you can open, not in the cell.
  • “Only here” is a blue sparkle caption under the row name, and a filter chip that shows only those rows (9 today). No pills inside cells.
  • Spotlight column: paper with a blue hairline and a faint blue wash under the header, plus the app mark. The rest of the page stays neutral.
  • Fair rows stay in: “Runs the code” and “Math and highlighting” are shown even where we tie or lose. That honesty is what makes the ticks believable.
  • Phone: a native picker (“ipynb viewer vs Colab ▾”) chooses the rival. Each row is its name over two side-by-side cells, so phrases have room and never wrap.

Exception to DESIGN.md

  • This page adds a tick colour token. It is used only for small marks, the hairline and captions, never a large fill. DESIGN.md would gain one line saying so.
